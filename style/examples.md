# House style

The show: "The Ripple Effect" (previously "Real Talk with Dave & Marshall"), hosted by Dave and Marshall.
Tagline: "Healing Through Laughter." 🎙️
Honest conversations and real stories with guests from all walks of life: mental health, resilience,
business, relationships, leadership, personal development, recovery and overcoming adversity.

Tone: warm, honest, funny. The laughs sit alongside serious topics, so the humour is always with people,
never at someone's struggle (mental health, addiction, recovery, grief).

Welcome to Episode 30 of Real Talk with Dave & Marshall.

Thirty episodes ago, we started this podcast with one goal: to create a space for honest conversations and
